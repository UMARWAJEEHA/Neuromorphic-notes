# Hardware Implementation of Spiking Neural Networks

## Problem Addressed
Traditional neural networks are not hardware-efficient for edge and embedded systems.

## Hardware Perspective
- Event-driven computation
- Sparse activity reduces energy usage
- Timing-based information encoding

## Architectural Challenges
- Precision vs power trade-offs
- Memory access patterns
- Scalability of neuron count

## Key Takeaways
Spiking neural networks are **naturally aligned with low-power electronic systems**,
making them suitable for embedded and real-time automation.

## Research Direction
Further exploration into **custom circuits and accelerators** is essential to fully
realize SNN efficiency benefits.
