# Event-Driven Processing in Neuromorphic Systems

## What Is Event-Driven Processing?
Computation occurs only when meaningful events (spikes) are generated, rather than
at every clock cycle.

## Electronics Perspective
- Reduced switching activity
- Lower dynamic power consumption
- No reliance on global clock signals

## Comparison with Clock-Driven Systems
| Aspect | Clock-Driven | Event-Driven |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| Power Usage | High | Low |
| Latency | Fixed | Adaptive |
| Efficiency | Low for sparse data | High |

## Relevance to Intelligent Automation
Event-driven processing enables **real-time, low-power decision making**, critical
for edge AI and autonomous electronic systems.
