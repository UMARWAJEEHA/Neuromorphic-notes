
## Topics Covered

### 1. Neuron Circuit Implementations
- Analog neuron circuits (integrate-and-fire models)
- Digital neuron implementations
- Mixed-signal neuron designs

### 2. Synaptic Hardware
- Memristive devices as synapses
- Programmable weight storage
- Plasticity mechanisms in hardware

### 3. Neuromorphic Architectures
- Asynchronous and clockless systems
- Event-based interconnects
- Scalable neuromorphic cores

### 4. Power and Efficiency
- Low-power design techniques
- Sparse event-driven computation
- Energy efficiency compared to conventional processors

## Focus Perspective
This work approaches neuromorphic systems from an **electronics and
hardware architecture perspective**, rather than biological modeling.

The goal is to understand how brain-inspired principles can be
implemented using **practical electronic systems**.

## Future Work
- Analysis of analog vs digital neuromorphic designs
- Study of memristor-based crossbar arrays
- Exploration of hardware–software co-design for SNNs
